## Task

Choose **ONE** data science project of your choice (e.g., from "Data Entrepreneurship in Action I" or another project — can be the same project as Assignment #1, or a different one). **Alternatively, you can choose a hypothetical case study.** Prepare a report evaluating the **IP law implications** of that project.

### Required Elements of the Report

1. A brief statement of the relevant facts of the project.
2. An analysis of whether the project involves data or (if relevant) other subject-matter that could be subject to one or more of: **database rights** (copyright and the sui generis database right), **copyright**, and **trade secret protection**.
3. An assessment of whether IP law could form a **legal barrier** to the successful completion of the project, including risks of **IP rights infringement**.
4. Advice on how identified legal barriers and/or infringement risks could be avoided/minimized, including possible alternative sources for obtaining/using data(bases) and (if relevant) other subject matter.

### General Guidance per Section

**I. Brief statement of the relevant facts of the project**
Explain what the project is: what data it generates/collects/uses, how, from where, and for what purpose — to give the examiner context.

**II. Analysis of IP rights protection**
Analyze whether the data or databases involved fulfill the criteria for protection under one or more of: database rights (copyright + sui generis database right), copyright, trade secret protection. If the project involves content protected by other IP rights (e.g., patents), include those too.

**III. Assessment of IP law implications**

- Can IP law form a legal barrier to completing the project? E.g., can IP rights prevent you from obtaining/using the needed data(bases)? Are specific steps required to obtain/use the data(bases) lawfully? Could this slow the project or reduce feasibility?
- **Infringement of IP rights:**
  - **(a)** If the project *does* involve data(bases) protected by one or more IP rights: analyze the risk of infringement.
    - If **yes** (risk exists): explain actions to prevent/minimize infringement risk. If risk can't be fully eliminated as currently planned, say so explicitly and suggest alternative avenues/changes to the project. If the project involves **data mining**, also analyze risk of breaching contractual terms and how to prevent/minimize this.
    - If **no** (no risk): clearly explain why (e.g., applicable legal exceptions to IP rights). If data mining is involved, also address contractual-term breach risk as above.
  - **(b)** If the project does *NOT* involve IP-protected data(bases): clearly explain why. If data mining is involved, still analyze contractual breach risk and mitigation.

**IV. Advice**
Final conclusions and way ahead. What strategies could minimize/avoid legal barriers posed by IP law and/or infringement risks? Are there lawful alternative sources for obtaining data/databases — what are they, what steps are needed to use them, and are there risks/disadvantages to those alternatives?
