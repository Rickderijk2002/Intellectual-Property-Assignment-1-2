## Task

Choose **ONE** data science project of your choice (e.g., from "Data Entrepreneurship in Action I" or another project — can be the same project as Assignment #2, or different) and write a report analyzing the data protection law implications of that project.

### Four Required Points of Analysis

1. **Data & actors:** Which personal data are processed in the project, what the relevant actor roles are, and the applicable regime (GDPR; special or non-special categories of data).
2. **Principles:** Assessment of how the data protection principles are applied to the processing.
3. **Lawful basis:** Assessment of the lawful processing ground relied on for the processing of personal data.
4. **Advice:** Advice on possible compliance issues and improvements through alternatives and technical measures.

### General Guidance per Point

1. **Identify and describe:**
   - Whether/which personal data are processed
   - Whether the special categories of data regime applies
   - Actor roles under GDPR (data subject, controller, processor) — with reasoning for controller/processor determination

2. **How data are processed:**
   - State the purpose of processing (purpose limitation principle)
   - Explain how each data protection principle applies

3. **Legal basis:**
   - Identify which processing ground is used
   - Apply the criteria of that ground — why it works (or potential problems with relying on it)

4. **Advice:**
   - Final conclusions and way forward
   - Conclude on GDPR compliance (or non-compliance)
   - Advise on alternatives: using non-personal data instead, or technical protection measures
